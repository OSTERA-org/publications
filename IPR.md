# Intellectual Property Rights

This file summarizes how intellectual property rights (IPR) apply to the
documents found in this repository. It is a summary for convenience only. The
OSTERA IPR and Copyright Policy is the authoritative text, and where this file
and the policy differ, the OSTERA IPR and Copyright Policy is authoritative.
Nothing here is legal advice.

* IPR and Copyright Policy: https://ostera.org/policies/
* Working Methods: https://ostera.org/policies/

## Patent Licensing Obligations by Document Type

Whether a document is subject to the patent licensing obligations of the IPR and
Copyright Policy depends on its type. The type code appears in each document's
identifier and filename (for example, `TS` in OSTERA TS 1000).

| Code | Document Type | Patent Licensing Obligations |
|---|---|---|
| STD | Standard | Yes |
| TS | Technical Specification | Yes |
| SUP | Supplement | Same as the document it supplements |
| TN | Technical Note | No |
| TP | Technical Paper | No |
| TR | Technical Report | No |
| PD | Policy/Process Document | Not applicable |

Errata and corrigenda carry the same obligations as the document they correct.
Documents without patent licensing obligations may still be subject to
copyright and other IPR obligations.

OSTERA has no official preference among licensing terms beyond what the policy
states (IPR and Copyright Policy). Working Groups are encouraged to
prefer technologies with no known IPR claims, or with royalty-free licensing
commitments, but a document in this repository is not guaranteed
to be implementable royalty-free.

## IPR Disclosures

Participants in OSTERA must disclose IPR they believe covers, or may ultimately
cover, technology under discussion. Anyone else who knows of
such IPR is strongly encouraged to disclose it as well.

* **To make a disclosure,** follow the instructions at https://ostera.org/ipr/

IPR disclosures and licensing declarations are never included in the documents
themselves.

The absence of a disclosure does not mean that no IPR covers a document, or that
none will be disclosed in the future. OSTERA takes no position on
the validity, scope, or applicability of any disclosed IPR.
